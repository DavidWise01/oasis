# U1 / Du0 — Authority / Observed-Fact Layer 00

Status: WORLD-HISTORY SCAFFOLD / APPEND-ONLY

This layer records what people in each period would treat as "fact" because it was:
1. directly observed,
2. inherited from trusted authorities,
3. embedded in scripture / sacred interpretation,
4. or institutionalized by calendars, law, education, and ritual.

It does **not** assert that every accepted belief was physically correct.

## Core distinction

OBSERVED FACT:
what a person can repeatedly see or measure.

AUTHORITY FACT:
what a trusted priest, scholar, ruler, book, school, or institution says is true.

SCRIPTURAL FACT:
what a community treats as revealed truth, including later interpretations layered onto the text.

MODEL FACT:
what reliably predicts useful outcomes even if its underlying explanation is wrong.

The same statement can occupy more than one class.

## Gilgamesh / early Mesopotamia

Observed:
- fire burns,
- bitumen/oil burns,
- seasons recur,
- Moon changes phase,
- stars and wandering lights move,
- floods destroy settlements,
- the ground is solid and apparently unmoving.

Authority:
- king, temple, priesthood, omen tradition.

World-model:
- Earth/Terra is the stable reference beneath all observation.
- sky events are meaningful because the sky is ordered.

Mythic records function as compressed causal memory:
sky fire, flood, ark/vessel, divine or royal order.

---

## Temple astronomy

Observed:
- regular lunar cycles,
- repeated planetary paths,
- eclipses,
- solstices/equinoxes,
- seasonal stellar recurrence.

Authority:
- temple tables,
- trained scribes,
- inherited astronomical records.

Accepted fact:
the heavens are ordered and can be predicted.

This is where prediction begins to confer authority:
if the table predicts the eclipse, the table's larger cosmology gains credibility too.

---

## Greek natural philosophy

Observed:
- Earth feels stationary,
- objects fall toward the ground,
- stars rotate nightly,
- planets wander against fixed stars.

Authority:
- philosophical schools,
- Aristotle,
- later mathematical astronomers.

Accepted physical picture:
Earth is central and terrestrial/heavenly motion obey different rules.

Competing fact:
Aristarchus-style heliocentrism exists, but everyday experience does not make a moving Earth obvious.

---

## Ptolemaic period

Observed/model fact:
Ptolemaic geometry predicts planetary positions reasonably well for its era.

Authority:
- mathematical astronomy,
- scholarly tradition.

Accepted fact becomes:
Earth is central AND the complicated sky can still be calculated.

Important distinction:
successful prediction protects the framework even when the framework is not physically fundamental.

---

## Biblical / late antique Christian layer

The Bible does not present a technical Ptolemaic astronomy textbook.
But communities read passages using the ordinary cosmology of their era.

Authority stack:
- scripture,
- Church interpretation,
- inherited Greek natural philosophy,
- educated astronomical tradition.

In-world accepted picture:
- Earth is stable,
- heavens are ordered,
- creation has purpose,
- humanity occupies a meaningful place within it.

Later interpreters may treat geocentric assumptions as if they are scripturally secured, even where the text itself is poetic, phenomenological, or nontechnical.

That distinction matters:
TEXT != EVERY LATER INTERPRETATION OF TEXT.

---

## Medieval western layer

Observed:
- same naked-eye heavens,
- practical calendars work,
- Earth still feels motionless.

Authority:
- scripture,
- Church,
- Aristotle,
- Ptolemy,
- university commentary,
- inherited manuscripts.

Accepted "fact" is therefore layered:

direct observation
+
successful old model
+
sacred authority
+
institutional repetition

This makes geocentrism resilient without requiring everyone to be stupid or dishonest.

---

## Islamic astronomy layer

Observed:
- increasingly precise planetary and stellar data.

Authority:
- translated Greek works,
- Qur'anic / theological traditions,
- mathematical astronomers,
- observatories and instruments.

Accepted facts become more contested:
Ptolemy is authoritative but correctable.

Methodological shift:
TRUST AUTHORITY
but
CHECK THE SKY.

That is a major transition in the Terra history.

---

## Scholastic / pre-Copernican Europe

Observed:
- accumulated anomalies,
- improved navigation,
- better timing,
- more mathematical sophistication.

Authority:
- Bible,
- Church fathers,
- Aristotle,
- Ptolemy,
- universities,
- commentaries.

The accepted world is now a compatibility stack:
if new observation conflicts with one layer, scholars try to reconcile it with the others before discarding the center.

This is "gentle join" applied historically.

---

## Copernicus

New model claim:
Earth moves around the Sun.

Observed at the time:
not yet an obvious naked-eye fact.

Authority conflict:
- traditional natural philosophy says Earth is stationary,
- scriptural interpretations are often read in stationary-Earth language,
- Copernicus offers mathematical reorganization.

So heliocentrism begins as a MODEL FACT before becoming broadly accepted as a PHYSICAL FACT.

---

## Tycho

Observed:
extremely precise planetary positions.

Authority:
data itself begins gaining authority.

Tycho's hybrid system shows the historical split cleanly:

OBSERVATION can be shared
while
INTERPRETATION remains contested.

---

## Kepler

Observed/data:
Tycho's measurements.

Model fact:
ellipses fit Mars better than perfect circles.

Authority change:
geometry yields to fit.

The old statement:
"heavenly motion must be perfect circles"

loses authority to:
"the measured orbit is elliptical."

---

## Galileo

Observed through telescope:
- mountains / irregular terrain on Moon,
- Jupiter has moons,
- Venus has phases,
- the Milky Way resolves into many stars.

This creates a new authority class:
INSTRUMENT-MEDIATED OBSERVATION.

Contention is no longer only book versus book.

It becomes:

BOOK / TRADITION
vs
INSTRUMENT
vs
INTERPRETATION OF WHAT THE INSTRUMENT SHOWS

Galileo's conflict becomes explosive because the center is tied to multiple authority systems at once.

---

## Historical rule for U1 / Du0

At every period, record four lanes:

OBSERVED
AUTHORITY
SCRIPTURE / MYTH
MODEL

Then ask:

which statements agree?
which conflict?
which institution has power to declare the conflict resolved?

That prevents the world history from flattening "fact" into one thing.

## Compact syntax

F[o] = observed fact
F[a] = authority fact
F[s] = scripture / sacred interpretation
F[m] = model fact

Example, pre-Galileo:

F[o] :: Earth feels still
F[a] :: Aristotle/Ptolemy support central Earth
F[s] :: prevailing interpretations fit a stable Earth
F[m] :: geocentric tables predict useful positions

Galileo introduces:

F[o+] :: telescope exposes new structure

and suddenly all four lanes no longer cohere automatically.
