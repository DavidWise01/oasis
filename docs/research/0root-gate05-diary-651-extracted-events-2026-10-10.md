# 0ROOT Gate 05 — Extracted AD -651 / BM 32312 observations
Date: 2026-10-10
Text source: https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/adsd/adart1/X106510/html
Digital corpus: Astronomical Diaries Digital (ADsD), based on Sachs & Hunger with editorial collation.
Museum identifier: BM 32312 / S+76-11-17,2040 / Oracc X106510.
Year: 652–651 BCE; astronomical year label -651 (not the same as a Julian calendar date).

## Line-specified records
1. Obverse col i line 1: Moon first visible in cloud; lunar visibility, bright/high reading tentative. Babylonian day unrecovered from broken line; Julian date unassigned.
2. Obverse col i line 2: On day 1, solar halo; overcast night and rising river level also recorded. Month label partly broken; absolute Julian date unassigned.
3. Obverse col i lines 7-8: Planetary Mercury/Saturn appearance/disappearance and observer explicitly notes he did not watch because of overcast conditions. Status = predicted/reported but NOT directly witnessed; do not label verified visual event.
4. Reverse col iv line 7: On day 5, Mercury first appeared in east in Pisces. Original month context reconstructed from diary, Julian conversion left unresolved.
5. Reverse col iv lines 14-16: On day 19, Venus near Mars in Aries, described as ten fingers behind Mars; day 20 Mars near a part of Aries, and Jupiter in lunar halo. Reported celestial relative positions, not exact modern angular coordinates.

## Extra witnessed environmental record
Reverse col iv line 20: Day 28, little rain and red? rainbow stretching east; wording uncertain (editorial question mark). Scholarly analysis: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5127895/

## Explicit constraints
- Lines 7-8 mention overcast and not watching; do not count them as independent direct visual confirmations.
- The line readings may be damaged, uncertain, or reconstructed (brackets/??); preserve that uncertainty.
- The original tablet's dates are Babylonian lunar-calendar dates. No automatic Gregorian-style leap-day adjustment. Need historical month intercalation and astronomy to transform to Julian dates.
- Secondary reconstructed Julian dates for Diary -651 have unsettled offsets; https://caeno.org/pdf/Diary%20651_Date%20translation%20table.pdf discusses discrepancies and is NOT the primary reading.
- An observed Venus–Mars positional relationship demonstrates multichannel reporting, not that the ancient author used modern State/Space operators or A.E.O.N.
- This extraction is documentary: no ephemeris computation was performed and no physical quantum/gravitational claim tested.
