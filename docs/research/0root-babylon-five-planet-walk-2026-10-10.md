# 0ROOT Babylon: full naked-eye planetary clock walk
Date: 2026-10-10
Source distinction: dated observations vs rounded modern synodic periods vs A.E.O.N. hypothesis.

## Evidence root
- Venus Tablet of Ammisaduqa (K.160), later cuneiform copies of observations attributed to the Old Babylonian reign ca 17th century BCE; ~21 years of Venus first/last visibility. Dating and copy accuracy are debated.
- British Museum artifact discussion via Google Arts & Culture: https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/cuneiform-tablet-with-observations-of-venus/uQGtUbfuWfzvLQ?hl=en
- A Babylonian astronomical almanac from 31–32 CE records the five naked-eye planets and lunar/eclipsing phenomena; this attests later comprehensive coverage, not a Venus-first discovery chronology.
- Metropolitan Museum primary catalog: https://resources.metmuseum.org/resources/metpublications/pdf/The_World_between_Empires_Art_and_Identity_in_the_Ancient_Middle_East.pdf (catalog #177).
