# 0ROOT — earliest attested astronomical and astrological clocks
Date: 2026-10-10
Status: source-oriented evidence catalogue, *not* a claim of shared origins or diffusion.

## Distinctions
- Sky observation predates writing; its beginning cannot currently be dated.
- Astronomical calendar: counting repeatable solar/lunar/stellar events.
- Celestial omen astrology: interpreting observed sky phenomena as meaningful signs.
- Personal natal horoscope: later development; should not be projected back onto earliest calendars.

## Anchors
1. Nebra Sky Disc (Central Europe), dating approximately second millennium BCE, buried around 1600 BCE; concrete visualization of celestial phenomena and possible lunisolar calendrical practices. This is **not** the beginning of all astronomy or an attested horoscope.
   Sources: https://www.landesmuseum-vorgeschichte.de/en/nebra-sky-disc ; https://www.landesmuseum-vorgeschichte.de/en/nebra-sky-disc/the-phases-of-the-sky-disc
2. Mesopotamian celestial omen literature, including Enuma Anu Enlil. Surviving Neo-Assyrian and Late Babylonian copies document celestial divination; the tradition was assembled over earlier centuries, and surviving copies must not be confused with the date of first invention.
   Sources: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_K-4292 ; https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_1880-0617-618
3. Babylonian zodiac and personal horoscopes are later developments than early sky observation and celestial omen traditions.
   Source: https://resources.metmuseum.org/resources/metpublications/pdf/The_World_between_Empires_Art_and_Identity_in_the_Ancient_Middle_East.pdf
4. Dresden Maya Codex: 260-day divination calendar and astronomical tables. A pre-Columbian, geographically distinct development; chronology alone does not establish borrowing.
   Sources: https://www.slub-dresden.de/en/explore/manuscripts/the-dresden-maya-codex/content ; https://www.slub-dresden.de/en/explore/manuscripts/the-dresden-maya-codex/calendar

## A.E.O.N. overlay — clearly hypothetical
Define event stream E(n) with Earth-based observer; append immutable dated observations; calculate independent phases with integer modular counters; compare predictive alignment. Treat these as *analogies*, not ancient encoding of A.E.O.N.

Root chain: observer -> lunar/solar observations -> recurrence count -> omen interpretation or predictive astronomical calendar -> audited ledger.
Do not infer Mercury -> Venus -> Earth -> Mars order of historical discovery without dated evidence.
