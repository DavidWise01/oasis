04 / QCD BOUNDARYWHAT THE COLORS ACTUALLY MEAN


In quantum chromodynamics (QCD), red, green and blue name three quantum charge indices, not human-visible colors. Protons carry flavor content uud; neutrons carry udd. Both are color-singlet baryons.


QCD has an eight-dimensional gluon gauge field associated with SU(3). KET's eight mesh lanes can display eight indexed positions without implying that those lanes literally are gluons.
