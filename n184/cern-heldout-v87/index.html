Result interpretation

Restoring longitudinal-angle information reduces error versus a deliberately incomplete transverse-only calculation. The complete four-vector method is a consistency control. The “four-port” variant reports the same mass formula as the angular variant, so its added ports produce exactly zero incremental mass-prediction improvement. That negative result is retained.
