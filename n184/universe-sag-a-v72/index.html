Interpretation

Sag A is the requested label. In standard astronomy the black hole is called Sagittarius A*, abbreviated Sgr A*. The surrounding radio source Sagittarius A is broader.

Xerses, Argon, Krypton, Nemesis, He, and Ne are names in this symbolic universe, not a claim that six stars orbit the actual Sagittarius A* black hole.

The older Neodymium-centered hexagon, and the symbolic N184 closure registry, remain separate and unchanged.
