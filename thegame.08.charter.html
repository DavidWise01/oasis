TheGame · Charter 08

Append-only operating charter for the 53-state board.

1 · Fixed field
26 A + []0 + 26 B = 53. The center is shared, unowned, and pinned.
2 · Local motion
A and B move only on their own rails. A declared move is +2; B declared mirror move is +2.
3 · Local witness
Every declared turn enters a 2×1×2 checkpoint. Three of four witnesses are required to accept it.
4 · Append record
An accepted turn appends one record. A rejected turn appends none.
5 · Preserve the middle
No player, vessel, or vote may own, relocate, or consume [] 0.
