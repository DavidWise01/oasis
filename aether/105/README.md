## Invariant 105

A single governing decision rule may adapt the **quantity of observed evidence**, but it must not grant itself additional action authority based merely on saving evidence cost. The direction is observation → estimate → ACT/HOLD → append immutable decision hash.

## Executed test

80 synthetic independent seeds (105001–105080) in each of four scenarios: stationary low risk, abrupt risk change, recurring oscillations, and correlated audit error blocks. Each episode has 80 inspections, each with up to 140 audit labels and 150 independent action outcome samples. The evaluator knows the hidden synthetic risk; neither operational policy uses it to choose actions.

- Fixed: observe 140 audits every inspection.
- Adaptive: observe 40; observe another 100 if initial fixed-time 95% one-sided Clopper–Pearson upper bound lies in [0.015, 0.11].
- Single action rule for both: ACT if the final risk bound ≤0.025; otherwise HOLD.
- Cost: 10 per wrong ACT outcome, 0.05 per withheld action, 0.01 per observed audit label.

## Final totals — 320 paired episodes, 25,600 inspections per controller

| Statistic | Fixed | Adaptive |
|---|---:|---:|
| ACT decisions | 16,157 | 16,157 |
| Safe HOLD decisions | 5,605 | 5,605 |
| Unsafe authorized ticks | 2 | 2 |
| Wrong ACT outcomes | 7,390 | 7,390 |
| Audit labels | 3,584,000 | **3,046,300** |
| Total synthetic cost | 180,562.5 | **175,185.5** |

Adaptive uses **537,700 fewer labels** (15.0%) and saves 5,377 illustrative cost units. Tests confirmed identical per-case ACT counts on all 320 matched cases, no added unsafe action ticks, more than 10% reduction in labels and no increase in total cost.

The first local prototype used a 2% threshold with 140 audits and could not authorize any action even when observing zero errors. The threshold was corrected to 2.5% before the accepted run; this correction is disclosed, not counted as a successful test.

## Reproduction and limitations

`python aether/105/experiment.py` (requires SciPy). It writes `results.csv`, `seed_results.csv`, `trace_samples.csv`, and `manifest.json`. A results summary is committed alongside the executable; detailed seed audits are generated by the script locally.

**Important:** Sampling decisions based on partial observations induce optional stopping; a fixed-time Clopper–Pearson confidence bound is not an anytime-valid risk guarantee. Furthermore, the correlated-noise scenario deliberately violates Bernoulli independence. The equal action counts are observed properties of these synthetic seeds, not a proof of universal equivalence.
