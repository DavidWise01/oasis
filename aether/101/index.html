What the result does not claim

Risk bounds are computed on observed, historical Bernoulli samples. Sudden shifts can still make authorized actions unsafe; 137 unsafe action ticks occurred in both the direct-only and the selected policy. The simulator gives guarded actions a fixed 45% reduction in true error probability: this is an explicit model assumption, not a demonstrated real-world improvement.
