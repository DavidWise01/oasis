The explicit trust boundary

The trusted-key assumption is supplied by the simulation. A valid HMAC proves that a packet matches data signed by the key holder, not that measurement sources are independent, accurate or uncompromised. Independence certification is assumed truthful at acquisition, not independently established. This is not a protocol for quorum, distributed agreement, or Byzantine resistance.
