### Binding rules (proposed, not a discovery)

- `LEVIATHAN`: a single **mnemonic ancestral referent** / Gen-0 candidate at the start of this model's lineage, not proof that a creature so named existed or that all extant life originated during one specific ice age.
- `att`: retained literally and **unexpanded** until its meaning is supplied.
- `1250 atm`: chosen pressure parameter inherited from v0.1. Not a measured pressure at Challenger Deep (roughly 1100 atm) and not independently sufficient to fix depth or temperature.
- `deep see under plank`: user's literal placement. An ice-covered deep-sea or enclosed/subsurface water environment is a **tentative modeling interpretation**; `plank` is not silently converted into `Planck length`, `plankton`, ocean crust, or ice. Its precise boundary type is left open.
- `N−`: model's nitrogen-centered ancestral **chemical/evolutionary branch**, not proof of actual nitrogen-backbone terrestrial life. Terrestrial life uses carbon-based organic chemistry but depends on nitrogen-rich amino acids and nucleic acids.
- `118..250`: INCLUSIVE index interval of 133 entries; `118` (oganesson) is recognized, while `119..250` are 132 unverified model candidate element slots. These slots are an extrapolation/search range, **not a periodic table of experimentally confirmed elements** and not necessary precursors of terrestrial life.

It does not imply those physical scales obey one identical empirical force equation, nor that `i` is an additional network node.

## Geological evidence boundary

- Earth formed ~4.5–4.6 Ga; microbial life certainly predates the well-established Huronian glaciations.
- Huronian glaciation intervals were roughly 2.4–2.1 Ga (NOAA); Cryogenian Snowball Earth intervals roughly 720–635 Ma (NASA). Neither identifies the moment or pressure at which life began.
- An ice-covered early prebiotic ocean is a scenario worth exploring, not an observed ancestor.
- Nitrogen matters for real origin-of-life chemistry; carbon remains the molecular backbone of all confirmed terrestrial life.
