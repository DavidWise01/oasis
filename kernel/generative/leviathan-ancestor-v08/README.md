### Four distinct operators

1. **ACTIVE** — a living instance can continue running its own local LIFE ENGINE while other branches operate; activity is not a single global life token.
2. **DIVERGE** — one active parent can give rise to more than one active descendant; birth **does not** imply parent death. Multiple generations may coexist.
3. **CONVERGE** — independent lineages can acquire the **same functional trait or solution**; convergence is not automatically sexual reproduction, hybridization, ancestry merging, or physical fusion. Those would be separate user-defined operations.
4. **DIE / RECYCLE** — a local life terminates and writes its append-only witness. The dying instance does not terminate siblings or parents. The parentage record remains accessible even after an individual dies.

The user's **1 judge / 2 players** applies per local board, **not** to a limit of two active individuals globally. Existing **3 attempts + 1 reserve** rules remain intact; a new death detector, selection metric or global lockstep is **not** invented.
