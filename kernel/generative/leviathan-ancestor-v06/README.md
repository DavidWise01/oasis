## Ancestral condition and side branches

`att :: 1250 atm` is a proposed user-defined root condition, **not** measured at 4.6 Ga. `−1100 atm` is a KET *signed coordinate*; real deep ocean ambient pressure is **positive absolute** and pressure cannot be converted to a unique Kelvin reading. `N−` and `O+` represent ocean and oxygen-associated ecological settings, not confirmed nitrogen-backbone vs oxygen-backbone biologies. Proposed comet catastrophe and LEVIATHAN, diatom, tardigrade as only survivors remain mnemonic archetypes, not literal paleontological reconstruction. Diatoms/tardigrades are not direct human ancestors.

The deep high-pressure ice-covered start is hypothetical. Oxygen rose long before the Devonian vertebrate shore transitions (~370 Ma), and fossils/molecular trees—not body mass—establish descent.
