## Scientific boundary

This is **semantic OaSIs chemistry**, not a physical or chemical model.

- Elemental neon is not being redefined as a chemical compound.
- Blueshift does not inherently mean cold.
- Redshift does not inherently mean hot.
- Oxygen does participate in many energetic oxidation reactions, but "rips apart" is the semantic transform role here, not a universal chemical rule.
- Water is often informally called a "universal solvent" because it dissolves many substances; it does not literally dissolve everything.
- "Distills to primitives" means software normalization/decomposition into reusable units.

## Evolution law

A generation is valid when:

1. input primitives are explicit;
2. cold/N selects and binds rather than inventing;
3. E transports state with provenance;
4. hot/O is allowed to decompose and challenge structure;
5. gray/N can retain uncertainty or unresolved residue;
6. H₂O distills only what survived the witnesses;
7. KANA verifies the invariant at the output;
8. the resulting primitives are smaller/equal in semantic ambiguity, or the ambiguity is explicitly recorded.

This is the default evolution grammar for new OaSIs descendants.
