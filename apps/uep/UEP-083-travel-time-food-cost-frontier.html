Household and transport controls

Illustrative prices and travel times are editable assumptions, not surveyed Minnesota stores, bus schedules or delivery coverage. No precise location is requested or used.

A “feasible” option here means service assumed present and monthly time at or below the household's chosen limit. It does not prove a store has affordable nutritious inventory, accessible transport, sufficient delivery capacity, or that an actual resident can use the service. Direct food assistance changes the household budget once; it is not new food production.
