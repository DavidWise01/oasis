Phase VII · 5/10 · Multi-resource and distributed regional governance

Single-period synthetic model. Water L, food kg, energy kWh, materials kg remain separate. Regeneration is capped by storage. Local essential withdrawal respects permanent floor and local access. Only fully served regions may donate stock above the declared future reserve, capped by export capacity. Direct lossless transfers serve recipient essential deficits subject to import, access and route ceilings. Transfers are immediately consumed by recipients, not stored. Deterministic maximum flow per resource; no shared transport budget, transshipment, conversion, money, or dynamic regeneration feedback. Future reserves are supplied inputs rather than forward forecasts. No actual transfers.

Regional resource balance

Maximum aggregate delivery does not imply fairness. Existing local reserve deficits are preserved and not worsened by transfers; they are not necessarily repaired. Future reserve forecasts and prior 48 counterexamples are not rerun here. No actual transactions.
