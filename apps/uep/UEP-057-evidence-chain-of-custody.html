Custody experiment controls

Custody protections

Authority model: all inspectors and beneficiaries are fictional. Root names describe ancestry, not authenticated real-world independence.

An event's digest is a simple deterministic checksum to demonstrate linked records, not a cryptographic signature. A malicious party with control of the entire file could recompute it.

Do not enter real names, addresses, health facts, financial identifiers or other sensitive data. Internal exports are not encrypted. Public exports contain only approved aggregate/project fields from this synthetic prototype.
