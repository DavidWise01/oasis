1 · Economic evidence import

Paste CSV or load a local file. The app never uploads personal documents or transmits the file to a server. Do not input personal identifiers, bank account numbers, names of claimants, or private medical records.

No external verification performed. Even complete evidence packages are only ready for independent assessment; they are not confirmed assets, revenue, obligations, or payments.

2 · Intake classes and decision gates

Public asset title

Ownership, licensing, or public equity right. Needs a source document and an authority reference; an asserted title is not recognized automatically.

Realized public revenue

Cash receipt attributable to public rights or activity. Needs issuer, transaction evidence and a cash receipt reference.

Restitution obligation

Original event or separately recognized loss. Needs a legal or authoritative basis and source ancestry; not a deduction from UBI.

Completed disbursement

An actual universal or restitution payment record. Needs a transaction reference and authorization basis; sensitive personal details are excluded.

Evidence state

Ready-to-review packages are not automatically authoritative. This application has no connection to government finance, courts, public title registries or banking settlement systems.
