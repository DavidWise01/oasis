Independent Authority Attestation & Revocation Challenge

Require two distinct signing roles for a fresh authority challenge: a claimed financial issuer and a separately named registry reviewer. Verify precise signed evidence, a short nonce-bound lifetime, explicit revocation state, and source-ancestry conflicts. Even two valid signatures cannot bootstrap official trust when both public keys arrive in the same untrusted package.
