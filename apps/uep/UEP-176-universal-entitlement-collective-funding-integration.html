Phase VII · 6/10 · Universal entitlement and collective funding integration

Single synthetic period. Regional physical allocations are supplied from the UEP-175 demonstration, not recalculated by its network engine. Equal per-person UBI entitlement; constrained funding distributed as equal integer cents plus one-cent bonuses in stable regional ID order. Region-level pooled purchasing is illustrative, not individual household distribution. Fixed prices and priority order water, food, energy, materials; no market response. Physical allocations are ceilings, not actual deliveries to consumers. Institutional contribution caps are assumed independent of UBI funding; proposed collective contributions are not receipts or legal liabilities. No actual payments.

Monetary and physical scarcity

Counterfactual zero restitution requirement must leave entitlement, allocation, arrears and purchasing unchanged. No individual equity proof, reserve carryforward, actual UEP-175 network execution or endogenous fiscal feedback. Stable cent tie ordering may bias repeated periods.
