Adaptive Recovery Scheduling

Put a fixed dispatch schedule and an adaptive scheduler through identical 30-day recovery conditions. Every day has the same incoming food, cold-chain capacity, traffic and new household demand. The adaptive scheduler can change how many of the available vehicles it dispatches—but cannot create food, exceed the fleet, or spend extra public money.

Scheduling experiment

Adaptive policy is a deterministic greedy dispatch heuristic, not an optimal or trained agent. It observes current inventory, current demand, cash and traffic; it does not see future arrivals. Fixed policy uses the specified daily vehicle count. Comparisons show net cost as well as coverage: better service using more of an existing budget is not free improvement.

Conservation and fairness audit

Both schedules run from the same zero initial inventory, identical gross daily arrivals and household demand, equal fleet limits and equal cash ceilings. Exact calendar events are used by both, with no random-number advantage.

Interpretation boundaries

ClaimWhat the experiment actually establishes
Adaptive is always superiorNot established. A well-selected fixed schedule or different disruption can match or outperform the greedy heuristic
Adaptive saves moneyNot automatic. Higher utilization may increase total spending while reducing cost per meal; report both
Storage is freeStorage capacity is finite; storage refrigeration costs are excluded here and must be priced before a deployment claim
More meals equals nutrition securityMeal-equivalent counts cannot verify food safety, nutrient coverage, appropriate delivery or equitable household access
Public finance is unlimitedOne budget ceiling is enforced for both schedules; no hypothetical emergency borrowing is introduced
Resource recovery proves scarcity is artificialNo. It can reduce avoidable loss while physical scarcity, affordability constraints and regional inequality remain possible

Next natural bound — UEP-087: Adaptive Scheduling Under Unseen Disruption

Test a controller tuned under ordinary conditions against unfamiliar traffic, storage outages, erratic arrival patterns and shrinking public funding.
