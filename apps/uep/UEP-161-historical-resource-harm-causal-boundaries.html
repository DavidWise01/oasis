Phase VI · 1/10 · Historical resource harm and causal boundaries.

All records are synthetic. Event ID and effect ID are declared identifiers, not proof of distinct real harms. Each effect is allowed once per resource category. Damage and repair are hypothetical quantities in separate dimensions. Bounded attribution uses assumed basis-point fractions of remaining harm, not statistical confidence or validated causal inference. Hypotheses retain a zero-to-full unresolved envelope; excluded records contribute zero attributed harm. Repair is assumed proportional across causal shares. Economic benefit is contextual only and cannot determine causation or liability. Health service slots represent hypothetical repair service requirements, not physical injury counts.
