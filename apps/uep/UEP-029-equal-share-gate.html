01 · Where the money comes from

Modify independently collectible, legally authorized annual public revenue; no source gives its users a preferential entitlement.

The revenue inputs are hypothetical realized annual net receipts, not estimates of what can currently be collected. The model does not add capital gains without realization or count the same AI earnings twice.

02 · Who receives an equal share

Equal entitlement can still need different mechanisms for children, people lacking IDs, unbanked recipients, and people needing assistance. These inputs do not resolve residency law, guardianship, or fraud controls.

03 · Payment integrity without access discrimination

PayableHeld entitlementsReinvestment + administration

A person without a smartphone, bank account, internet connection, or AI subscription remains eligible under the chosen population rule. The model holds unpaid allocations rather than transferring them to wealthier or digitally connected participants. Real rules require an accessible alternative claim and payment channel.
