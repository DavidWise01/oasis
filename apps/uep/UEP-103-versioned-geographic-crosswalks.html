Reference data and scientific boundary

For actual tract-vintage changes, use a documented geographic relationship file or weighted crosswalk published for the appropriate geography, vintage and measurement. Start with U.S. Census geographic relationship files and TIGER/Line geography. A Census relationship file may have geographic area relationships rather than population-based weights: inspect documentation before assigning person counts.

No geographic boundary polygons or authoritative weight values are bundled. The lab cannot establish that an area-based weight approximates household or population redistribution.
