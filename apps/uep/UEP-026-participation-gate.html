Dual-ledger invariant: neither can erase the other

RESTITUTION LEDGER:
  established taking → obligation → independently determined compensation
  → amount paid → balance still owed

TECHNOLOGY DIVIDEND LEDGER:
  verified publicly owned asset → realized return → sustainable allocation
  → eligible person → entitlement → verified payment

RESTITUTION BALANCE - TECHNOLOGY DIVIDEND = INVALID OFFSET
unless a separately valid legal settlement explicitly provides otherwise.

Public equity, royalties and infrastructure earnings do not establish restitution amounts. An eligible person may have rights under both ledgers independently. An equal dividend is not a waiver of separate legal claims.
