Financial invariants and limitations

RiskNecessary distinction
Tail event exceeds reserve assetsClaims remain approved but unpaid; do not silently transfer liability to beneficiaries
Many regions fail in the same periodModel dependence explicitly; ordinary independent-event assumptions understate joint-tail exposure
Target capital based on finite trialsQuantile estimates are uncertain; rare unobserved disasters may exceed modeled maximum
Disputed or forged provider claimsOnly independently approved liability belongs in this distribution; disputed exposure needs a separate model
Protected service budgetIt is excluded from reserve assets and cannot be used to make modeled shortfalls appear solvent
Operational liquidityAn annual reserve calculation does not test intra-period cash timing or provider insolvency
ReconciliationStable claim identifiers must prevent duplicate reimbursements across institutions and reporting periods
