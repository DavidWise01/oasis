Restitution guardrails

ProblemEconomic rule
Past harmEstablish actual injury and remedy under applicable law; neither speculation nor headline allegations create an automatic payable claim
Loss of useState the lawful rate or valuation basis and date range, not an arbitrary compounding assumption
Same-harm compensationCredit actual prior compensation for that same injury to avoid duplicate recovery where legally appropriate
Universal essential servicesNever count the general universal floor as restitution for a distinct past injury
AI dividendNever treat equal participation in public AI income as payment for an individual's historical loss
Debt reliefKeep legally distinct debt reforms separate; specific debt cancellation can count only where it truly remedies the same injury and applicable law permits
Property restorationConsider return of original property, replacement or other legally authorized relief rather than assuming all harms can be priced accurately in cash
Public solvencyDisclose who must fund a remedy; do not conceal public obligations behind fictitious revenue or reduce the universal floor
