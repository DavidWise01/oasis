Phase VI · 7/10 · Restoration prioritization and temporal scheduling.

All data synthetic. Divisible projects have distinct declared harm IDs, unit-specific output, per-unit input coefficients (cents, kWh, labor-hours, material kg), and period throughput. Priority is descending urgency (0–3), declared ordinal delay consequence, consecutive zero-allocation periods, affected population, then lexical ID. This is a greedy policy, not a globally optimal or clinically validated schedule. Budgets reset each period; unspent amounts do not carry forward. Planned output reduces unscheduled workload only, not actual harm. No accepted output, actual payment, or additional physical damage inferred from delay.
