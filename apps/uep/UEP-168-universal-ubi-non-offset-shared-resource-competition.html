Phase VI · 8/10 · Universal UBI non-offset and shared resource competition.

One synthetic period; separate external UBI and restitution budgets in cents. One homogeneous shared input in liters, shared stock and throughput. Essential demand is reserved before repair, even if current UBI purchasing capacity cannot use that reservation. UBI paid is a simulated aggregate disbursement, not an individual entitlement ledger or actual transaction. Repair consumes liters per planned output unit; output is not accepted repair. Restitution budgets cannot draw on UBI funds. Fixed positive prices and coefficients, no carryforward or market response. Physical reservation is a policy assumption, not empirical proof.
