Phase V · 3/10 · Essential human needs and physical demand baselines.

One abstract planning period. Rows: resource, unit, essential quantity per person, available capacity, discretionary demand. Values are synthetic engineering fixtures, NOT medical, nutritional or public-health recommendations. Healthcare slots and shelter person-nights are period-specific service capacity, not stored inventory; an unused slot cannot automatically carry forward. Healthcare demand is a hypothetical planning scenario, not a claim that everyone needs an appointment every day.

Essential needs first

Resource categories remain separate. No price or income input determines essential demand. Aggregate fulfillment does not prove equitable individual access, quality, nutritional adequacy or clinical appropriateness.
