Restitution ≠ dividend

Restitution liabilities must be established and settled in a separately sourced ledger. The balance shown here excludes restitution claims and never marks those liabilities discharged.

REST-LEDGER: owed → validated → paid → outstanding
TECH-FUND: owned → earned → collected → reserved → dividend

A payment from TECH-FUND does not reduce REST-LEDGER.

Accounting guardrails

Cash is capped by invoiced amounts for this simplified demonstration. Negative claims, duplicate ownership IDs, missing bank evidence, or missing distribution authority are gated. This is a cash-flow experiment, not GAAP accounting or an audit opinion.
