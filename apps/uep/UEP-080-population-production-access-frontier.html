Three tests of any claim of scarcity

QuestionEvidence neededWhat remains unknown in this build
Is there too little physical production?Comparable regional output, sustainable yields, actual per-person needs, infrastructure and ecological budgetsGlobal access-gap headcounts alone do not establish this
Is capacity available but inaccessible?Local prices, incomes, connection costs, service coverage, quality, geography, waste and idle infrastructureThese indicators quantify deprivation, but not its exact cause in each place
Is control of supply contributing to deprivation?Ownership shares, market power, contract restrictions, price-cost margins and credible counterfactualsNeither concentration nor intention can be inferred from a gap figure alone

Choose one resource and one regional geography. Measure local production, transport, losses, affordability, service reliability and unmet need in compatible units. Only then test whether new capacity, public infrastructure, waste recovery or distribution reform is the binding constraint.
