Debt relief: separate timeline

Debt cancellation removes a stock of obligations. The estimated first-year interest avoided is only a simplified indicator: real payment savings depend on the repayment schedule, type of debt, variable rates, and whether the borrower would have repaid. Never add the full principal cancellation to every subsequent year's income.

Universal equivalence

Equal public entitlements need not force identical purchases. A debt-free or mortgage-free person cannot receive zero solely because another participant has debt. An eligible person with low power consumption should have another usable benefit route.
