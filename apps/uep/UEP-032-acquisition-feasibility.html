Feasibility decision

Capital recovery

Three objections to test

Acquisition cost: A $40B public stake requires financing or a legitimate asset exchange; public ownership does not appear simply because AI is profitable.

Displacement: Transfers from firms to the public can affect private reinvestment, prices, workers and investors; those changes are not captured by static NPV.

Cash versus benefit: Lower AI-driven prices help consumers but cannot automatically finance an income transfer.

Cash recovery over time

Discounted cumulative cash after public equity investment. Assumes no asset resale value or terminal value.
