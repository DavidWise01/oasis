2 · What the cryptography can and cannot prove

Signature integrity

A valid ECDSA signature shows that the supplied private key signed the exact canonical message bytes.

Challenge freshness

A nonce must equal the verifier's expected challenge; an issued/expiry window prevents expired assertions from passing.

Transaction identity

Event ID, period, cash transaction reference and integer cents must match exactly.

Public authority and settlement

A user-entered JWK is not a trusted bank or treasury key. Official key distribution, rights, and actual financial settlement remain externally unverified.
