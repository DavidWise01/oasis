System constraints

All parameters describe a synthetic network, not actual Minnesota food distribution, route reliability or nutrition security.

No choice among conflicting objectives is mathematically universal. A service floor is enforced first, and the selected tie-breaking rule is displayed. The “best” policy is therefore best only for this explicit, editable decision rule and model—not for all real households or ecological outcomes.
