The Universal Essential Floor

Begin with what every person needs. Housing, food, utilities, healthcare, mobility, connectivity and everyday necessities form a guaranteed monthly economic floor. Make access automatic and universal, not a discretionary prize or a means-tested cliff. People choose among cash, direct bill payment and portable service access.

No hidden eligibility cliff

Universal enrollment means the basic guarantee does not disappear when someone obtains employment, receives a raise, changes jobs or disputes a charge. Extra disability, location and care requirements can be added because equal dignity does not mean identical needs. A legal funding framework would still have to specify jurisdiction, coverage, residency and implementation.
