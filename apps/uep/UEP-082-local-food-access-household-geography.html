Household comparison controls

The county presets are illustrative, not observed county averages. USDA's low-food-access measures use spatial thresholds and tract-level data; they do not give the travel distance or grocery bill for any specific household.

Distances, price premiums, travel speeds, budgets and benefit values are illustrative user-controlled assumptions, not actual county measurements. “No vehicle” adds a separate transit-cost assumption; the calculation does not establish that a transit route or grocery delivery exists.

What we still cannot conclude

These examples cannot tell us whether Wright County or Hennepin County has “too many people,” whether any business deliberately restricts supply, or whether county-wide grocery availability meets all nutritional needs. Resolving those questions requires geographic data at tract or address scale, real household consumption data, actual store inventories and transport networks, price surveys, and ecological capacity measurements.
