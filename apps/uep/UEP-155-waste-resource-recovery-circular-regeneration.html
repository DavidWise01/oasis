Phase V · 5/10 · Waste, resource recovery and circular regeneration.

One homogeneous synthetic material in integer kg; energy in kWh. Previously accumulated waste is processed before new material demand, bounded by processing capacity and available recycling energy. Returned material is available for recycling next period only. Discarded is a maximum return quantity per period, not a measured behavioral rate. Delivered material not returned leaves the modeled circulating stock as retained material; processing loss leaves the recoverable material class, not physical existence. Energy is a separate exogenous budget, not generated by recycling.

Material lifecycle

Purple: usable stock · Orange: waste backlog · Teal: cumulative processing loss

All inputs synthetic. No actual recycling efficiency, physical feasibility or environmental benefit established. No financial transactions.
