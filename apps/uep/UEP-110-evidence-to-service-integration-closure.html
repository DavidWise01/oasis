4 · Acceptance boundaries

Source filesMust be sourced, byte-pinned, versioned and replayed; hash identity does not prove publisher authenticity
Crosswalk weightsMust be appropriate for the source units; method disagreement is not a statistical confidence interval
Provider capacityMust be current and independently confirmed at the date and location of proposed service
Unique completionsNeed observed, privacy-preserving delivery evidence; simulated attempts are not deliveries
Public moneyNeeds actual appropriation, ledger and reconciled accounting records, not hypothetical unit prices
