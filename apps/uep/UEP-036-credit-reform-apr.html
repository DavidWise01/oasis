What can legislation change?

Rate ceilings: Compare a nationwide APR ceiling with state laws and institution-specific rules. Specify which transactions and fees count toward the ceiling.

Rate increases: Define repricing notices, repricing reviews, penalty APR limits, and protections for existing balances.

Fees and collection: Limit or prohibit specified fees while preserving validation, correction, and due-process protections.

Access risk: Evaluate whether caps change credit approval, lines, rewards, annual fees, and availability of alternative lending.
