Experiment controls

All figures are hypothetical food-network parameters. Training traces and held-out scenarios are generated by explicit deterministic rules—not actual Minnesota operations or independent empirical validation.

The fixed benchmark is selected using three familiar cases only; held-out cases are never used in selection. The adaptive heuristic is frozen rather than trained. Its use of today's storage, demand and road conditions does not reveal tomorrow's arrivals. Equal ceilings do not imply equal actual spending: both throughput and cost are reported.

Next natural bound — UEP-088: Resource-Normalized Control

Replace simple throughput comparisons with a resource-normalized frontier. Require controllers to account for every delivered meal's cash, vehicle-hours, cold storage and reliability; test whether adaptive scheduling still adds value after equal-spend and equal-capacity normalization, including cases where fixed dispatch performs better.
