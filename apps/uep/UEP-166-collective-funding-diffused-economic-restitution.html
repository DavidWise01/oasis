Phase VI · 6/10 · Collective funding responsibility.

All amounts are synthetic integer cents. Institutions have declared retained economic benefit, available contribution capacity and continuity factor in basis points. Weight is benefit multiplied by continuity, rounded down. Positive weights participate in a capped iterative proportional allocation using integer arithmetic and deterministic ID ties. Contributions are proposed funding allocations, not legal liabilities, actual receipts or verified causation. Capacity is assumed separately available for restoration after essential commitments; the actual UBI ledger is not invoked. Zero-weight institutions are excluded from this particular funding rule, not exonerated. No individual descendants or personal debt are modeled.
