2 · Economic meaning of authentication

Economic identity

One event, period, transaction reference and amount; one declared cash receipt, not one receipt per reporter.

Authority ancestry

Each observation points to an authority with a root identifier and evidence reference. Shared roots do not multiply independent support.

Transaction consistency

Observed amount and transaction reference must match the original event. Conflicting observations hold the entire event.

External confirmation

True authentication requires independently checking the named authority and source documents outside this browser. No internal label can substitute.

Financial gate

Even corroboration-ready revenue is excluded from operationally authorized distributable funds until independently authenticated by a trusted external channel.
