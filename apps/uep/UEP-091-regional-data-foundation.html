What the source metadata establishes

The source links and their documented vintages are verified references. The county values themselves have not been downloaded or loaded here. USDA Food Environment Atlas indicators have differing reference years, and the Food Access Atlas now includes SRAM and LRAM with different retailer universes and underlying tract geographies. Feeding America's 2026 report estimates food insecurity using 2024 data, not 2026 county measurements.
