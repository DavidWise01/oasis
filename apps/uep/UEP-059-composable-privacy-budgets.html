Important boundaries
IssueRequirement


Individual appealExact authorized record changes belong in a confidential channel; a new public statistical release consumes a new budget allocation


Repeated queriesMemoization prevents repeated sampling of the same snapshot, but changing the dataset creates a new answer and new composition cost


Overlapping queriesPrivacy accounting must cover all public releases, not just obvious intersections; composition adds across distinct responses


Budget exhaustionRefuse further public responses until authorized governance establishes a fresh, justified budget


Noise and arithmeticNoisy counts may be fractional, negative or inconsistent across groups; do not silently treat them as exact payment totals


Public accountabilityPublish methodology, privacy expenditure and aggregate statistics without releasing private beneficiary evidence


Practical deploymentUse a trusted curator, reviewed query language, contribution bounds, durable accounting, secure entropy and audited access controls
