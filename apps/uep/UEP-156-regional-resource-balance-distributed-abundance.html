Phase V · 6/10 · Regional resource balance and distributed abundance.

One homogeneous synthetic resource in liters per planning period. Regions: ID, stock, essential demand, protected floor. Routes: origin, destination, transfer capacity. Local needs and floors are protected first. Deterministic maximum flow routes surplus directly to deficit regions; no intermediate transshipment, travel delays, losses or energy costs. Total fulfillment is maximized, not necessarily individual or regional equity. No real geographic or resource data.
