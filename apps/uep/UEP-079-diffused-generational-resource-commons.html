Resource accounting boundaries

ClaimNeeded distinction
“There are too many people.”Test resource-specific sustainable output against actual per-person needs; include regional ecology and nonhuman needs before inferring carrying capacity
“There is enough for everybody.”Check nutrition, potability, firm energy, housing suitability, transport and local capacity; aggregate quantities alone cannot guarantee access
“A small group controls supply.”Compare market concentration, price formation, property rights, usable idle capacity and legal access; concentration alone does not establish intentional restriction
“Technology solves scarcity.”Model energy, materials, water, land, time, maintenance and ecological impacts of new production
“The past generation took from us.”Measure diffused stocks, flows, exposure, inherited assets and opportunity over time—not guilt by birth cohort
“Recovery pays for itself.”Include the capital, labor, energy, ecological and distribution costs of recovering wasted or unused capacity
