Design a sufficient income

The model treats cash yield as already net of maintenance and investment expenses, but subtracts explicit further reserve and administration fractions. Sources of new capital must be established separately. Inflation escalates the target payment while nominal asset yield, royalties and additions remain unchanged unless you change assumptions.
