6 · Fixed-window and provenance controls

Operational contacts and hours can change. A listing should prompt verification, not automatic service claims. User-entered availability is not a reservation, and this simulator makes no appointment or service booking. Never submit actual household names, addresses or sensitive eligibility information to this demo.
