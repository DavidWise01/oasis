Essential-service production

The difference between a market bill and public delivery cost is not automatically a public cash dividend. Capital expenditure, depreciation, financing and overhead are modeled separately. The service benefit is only meaningful to recipients who actually need or use it; equivalent alternative entitlements require separate funding.

Debt treatment

Legislative design constraints

QuestionMechanism to examine
Was this charge legitimately incurred?Validation notices, dispute procedures, evidence standards, remedies for unauthorized charges
Who owns the obligation?Identify creditor, servicer, owner and applicable contract or statute
Who has authority to cancel it?Existing creditor powers, existing statutes, or newly enacted legislation subject to constitutional constraints
Who bears the fiscal or economic cost?Public purchase price, loss to public balance sheet, creditor loss, service costs, or reduced future revenue
Does the benefit recur?Separate outstanding principal cancellation from interest avoided, future fees, and monthly payment reductions

References: 15 U.S.C. §1692g: debt validation · CFPB dispute guidance · Biden v. Nebraska · Contracts Clause.
