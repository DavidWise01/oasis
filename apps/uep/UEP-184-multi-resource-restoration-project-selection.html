Phase VIII · 4/10 · Multi-resource restoration project selection

Single-period synthetic indivisible projects, at most 18. Water L, energy kWh, materials kg, labor hours and funds cents are separately bounded. Available budgets minus protected commitments define candidate capacity; any existing protection deficit blocks selection. Exhaustive subset search maximizes declared comparable harm units lexicographically by urgency tiers 3 to 0, then conservative gain in a common declared L/period water regeneration metric, then minimizes funds, water, energy, materials, labor and project count, then lexical IDs. Harm units are assumed nonoverlapping and comparable within this synthetic catalog; projects are independent, no shared prerequisites. Selected portfolio is a candidate only, not authorization. No actual transactions.
