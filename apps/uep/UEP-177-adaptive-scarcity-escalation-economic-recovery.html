Phase VII · 7/10 · Adaptive scarcity escalation and economic recovery

One synthetic resource and aggregate UBI ledger; observations supplied externally each period. Physical scarcity is unmet physical availability or reserve gap; monetary scarcity is purchasing gap or UBI funding gap. Both can coexist. Immediate escalation; consecutive clear observations required for release, reset by relapse. Exceptional restrictions affect only discretionary repair eligibility, never UBI claims or essential delivery. Normal state restores the supplied repair ceiling as a candidate, not authorization: temporal gate remains required. No real payments or policy enforcement.

Scarcity and recovery timeline

Thresholds are deliberately zero-gap and recovery duration is a policy choice. No forecast, causal attribution, physical intervention, prior module execution or actual economic recovery demonstrated. Candidate repair remains distinct from authorized repair.
