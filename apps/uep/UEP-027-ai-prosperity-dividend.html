Critical accounting separations

AI-generated GDP ≠ AI-generated incremental net surplus
Incremental surplus ≠ legally owned public revenue
Public revenue ≠ settled cash
Settled cash ≠ guaranteed permanent UBI

RESTITUTION ≠ TECHNOLOGY DIVIDEND
No technology-dividend payment cancels any separately established restitution claim.

AI may generate large consumer benefits by lowering prices, but lower costs are not automatically cash that can be transferred to households. A UBI also changes labor, prices, consumption and investment; this static model does not estimate those behavioral feedbacks.
