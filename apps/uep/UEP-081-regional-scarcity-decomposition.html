Regional supply inputs

All meal-equivalent numbers in this panel are invented, editable scenario inputs, expressed in millions of nutritionally adequate meal-equivalents annually. They are not official Minnesota production, consumption, waste, or hunger estimates.

Public signal: The Food Group reports 9,026,843 Minnesota food-shelf visits in 2025 across 470 TEFAP-participating food shelves. A visit is not a unique person, a meal, or a quantified unmet need. The Minnesota food-shelf visit figure does not establish the volume of total food available in Minnesota.
