Phase VII · 8/10 · Intergenerational capacity targets and civilizational inheritance

Four independent synthetic resource dimensions: water L, food kg, energy kWh, materials kg. Each generation inherits prior closing stock, regenerative rate, outstanding harm and separate monetary obligations. External restoration observations, regenerative gains and settlements are supplied, not authorized by this engine. Regenerative gains activate next generation; gain coefficient bounded to one rate unit per accepted repair unit as a synthetic assumption. No repair inputs are deducted here because external repair is assumed outside this inventory boundary. Essential withdrawals respect permanent floor; exogenous losses may breach it and are reported. Targets are declared policy, not validated sustainability thresholds. No actual transactions.
