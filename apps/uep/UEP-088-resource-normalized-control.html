More deliveries are not necessarily greater efficiency. Compare fixed and adaptive food-recovery dispatch on the same deterministic supply trace, then normalize by public spending, vehicle-hours, refrigerated inventory-days and unmet need. Test multiple matched budget ceilings to expose performance tradeoffs.

Equal limits are not necessarily equal actual expenditure. Resource dominance requires at least as many delivered meals while using no more cash, vehicle-hours and cold-storage occupancy, with at least one strict improvement. This criterion can yield no winner: tradeoffs are real. “Cold storage / meal-day” is illustrative and charged against the same operating budget.
