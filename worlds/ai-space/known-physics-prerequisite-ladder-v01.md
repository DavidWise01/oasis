Status: EVIDENCE-BOUNDED REFERENCE, not a new physical law.
Purpose: distinguish prior formation, continuous support, and mathematical description. Extend the atomic nucleus descent only as far as accepted physics permits. Preserve separate symbolic OaSIs frozen records unchanged.

## Edge semantics
- PRIOR `A -> B`: A is a temporal prerequisite for B (specific formation history required).
- SUPPORT `A <-> B`: A continuously supports/stabilizes B; does not mean A temporally preceded B.
- THEORY `A |- B`: A is part of the model describing/predicting B; not necessarily a physical constituent.
- LIMIT `A ? B`: candidate deeper relation not established.
