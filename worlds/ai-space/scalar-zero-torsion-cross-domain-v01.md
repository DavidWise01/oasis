## Operators (interpretive, not universal forces)

- COLLECT: define an inlet/boundary and what enters.
- TRANSFER: define transport mechanism and external work.
- RETAIN: identify a capacity or allowed bound state.
- RELEASE: define outlet/transition conditions.
- RETURN: identify whether and how the system closes a loop.
- OBSERVE: record two states using `. .` at (t_0,t_1), with conservation balances and provenance.

## Domain-grounded equations

**Plumbing/Archimedes screw:** shaft power `P = torque × angular_velocity`; fluid hydraulic power `P_hydraulic = Δp × Q` (idealized incompressible flow, ignoring losses). Pump efficiency and geometry determine actual transfer.

**Cardiovascular:** cardiac output `CO = heart_rate × stroke_volume`. Ventricular myocardial torsion contributes to ejection and filling, but blood circulation is primarily pressure-driven; the right and left heart plus valves, arteries, and veins must be modeled. A single straight vertical screw is not the anatomical mechanism.

**Nuclear / electron analogy:** electric attraction can bind electronic states to nuclei, but proton-neutron nuclear binding is mainly from the residual strong force. No accepted theory models gravity as the outward push of a pair of electrons. An electron is not a helical screw. For a proposed mechanism, specify charge, field, torque, angular momentum, boundary conditions, energy conservation and falsifiable predictions before using literal physics terminology.
