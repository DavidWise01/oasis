## 0. Historical boundary

The Temple Hymns are transmitted as a collection of **42 hymns** to temples/deities/cities. Modern scholarship also notes that the surviving textual tradition is later and fragmentary, and Enheduanna's authorship/editorship of the entire received collection is debated.

The ETCSL colophon closes the received composition with:

```text
compiler of the tablets :: En-hedu-ana
```

This file preserves the 42-node received corpus as the object being bound. The `4x10 + 2` geometry below is a **Root0/alchemical cipher distillation**, not an ancient attested division of the hymns.
