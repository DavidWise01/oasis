# 3. Core mythic geography

## Themiscyra

```text
THEMISCYRA
::
Amazon homeland / city
```

Classical sources place it near the Thermodon River on the Black Sea coast of Anatolia.

## Thermodon

```text
THERMODON
::
river / territorial anchor
```

The Thermodon is the recurring geographic marker for Amazon territory in Greek and Roman mythography.

## Doiantian plain / nearby cities

Apollonius and later mythographers place Amazon territory around the Doiantian plain and describe multiple Amazon settlements in the region.

**Status:** MYTHIC GEOGRAPHY.

# 4. Origin layer

Variants differ.

```text
ARES
+
HARMONIA or OTRERA traditions
->
AMAZONS / AMAZON QUEENS
```

Some classical traditions call the Amazons daughters or descendants of Ares. Otrera is a major queen/mother figure; another tradition pairs Ares with the nymph Harmonia.

**Status:** MYTH VARIANTS — preserve alternatives, do not collapse.

# 5. Named Amazons / queens

## Otrera

```text
OTRERA
::
early queen / mother figure
```

Associated in later mythographic tradition with Ares and with daughters including Hippolyta and Penthesilea.

## Hippolyta / Hippolyte

```text
HIPPOLYTA
::
queen
::
girdle of Ares
```

Her girdle is the object of Heracles' ninth labor in the standard mythic cycle.

## Antiope

```text
ANTIOPE
::
Amazon queen / princess
::
Theseus cycle
```

She is linked to Theseus in varying traditions: abduction, marriage, alliance, or conflict.

## Penthesilea

```text
PENTHESILEA
::
queen
::
Trojan War
::
Achilles
```

She comes to Troy as an Amazon warrior queen and is killed in battle by Achilles in the major literary tradition.

## Other named Amazons

Greek vase painting and literary catalogues preserve many additional names, including Iole, Aella, Philippis, Hippothoe, Xanthe, and others.

**Status:** names vary by source and period.

# 7. Amazonomachy — the recurring image

```text
GREEKS
VS
AMAZONS
=
AMAZONOMACHY
```

Amazonomachy becomes a major visual and political mythic theme in Greek art: vase painting, architectural sculpture, and monumental programs.

The Met preserves numerous Attic objects showing Heracles, Theseus, Achilles, and other Greeks fighting Amazons.

**Corpus relation:** repeated visual encoding of the same mythic opposition.

# 9. Herodotus branch — myth meets ethnography

Herodotus tells a story in which captured Amazons meet Scythian youths and become ancestors of the Sauromatae.

```text
AMAZON MYTH
->
SCYTHIAN CONTACT
->
SAUROMATAE ORIGIN STORY
```

This is an ancient ethnographic-origin narrative, not modern proof that the mythical Amazon nation literally existed.

Herodotus also describes Sauromatian women as riding, hunting, and fighting.

**Status:** ANCIENT ETHNOGRAPHIC TRADITION.

# 10. Archaeology boundary

Modern archaeology has found armed women in some Eurasian steppe burials.

That evidence can support the possibility that real warrior women helped shape Greek Amazon stories.

It does **not** establish:

```text
THEMISCYRA existed exactly as myth says
or
a women-only Amazon nation existed exactly as myth says
```

So:

```text
STEPPE WARRIOR WOMEN
:: HISTORICAL / ARCHAEOLOGICAL POSSIBILITY

GREEK AMAZONS
:: MYTHIC CORPUS

IDENTITY
:: UNRESOLVED
:: STOP
```

# 11. Mythic social structure

Classical authors give inconsistent accounts of Amazon society.

Recurring motifs include:

```text
women as warriors
women as rulers
men marginal / external / differently integrated
military training
horse culture
female lineage / queenship
```

Claims such as systematically killing all male infants are later variant motifs and should not be frozen as a universal Amazon rule.

**Status:** VARIANT TRADITION.
