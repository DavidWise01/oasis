Enheduanna served as high priestess of **Nanna**, the moon god at Ur.


Her best-known literary corpus is strongly associated with **Inanna**, the Sumerian goddess later identified with Ishtar.

Historical alchemy comes much later. The following is a **cipher overlay**, not a claim that Enheduanna practiced alchemy or intentionally encoded later alchemical metal correspondences.

Later alchemical traditions used:

So the overlay becomes:

