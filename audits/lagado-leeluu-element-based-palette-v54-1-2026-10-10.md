## Four newly fixed palette semantics
- **GREEN** = **oxygen-based** (O-inspired)
- **ORANGE** = **hydrogen-based** (H-inspired)
- **PURPLE** = **neon-based** (Ne-inspired)
- **BLUE** = **nitrogen-based** (N-inspired)

These are **oxygen-/hydrogen-/neon-/nitrogen-BASED** modeling categories, not assertions that the engraved glyph depicts an isolated atom of that physical element. The four other historical color lanes red, black, white and yellow are **unchanged and currently have no element interpretations from this correction**. Do not invent them. Earlier v45 eight-color classes and their source-atom assignments are kept fixed.
