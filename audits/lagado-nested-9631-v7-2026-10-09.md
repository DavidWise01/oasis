## Split semantics (explicit modeling assumptions)
The user-provided 1/3/6/9 fields are **nested signed operator registers**, not a 19-bit plaintext string. Preserve all 16 source bits independently; build a derived 3x3 nine-site ternary plane using nine overlapping 2x2 windows of the existing 4x4 glyph. A window with 0 or 1 occupied bits maps to -1, 2 maps to 0, and 3 or 4 to +1. The template `-1+ -0+ +1-` is compared by interpreting template 1 as positive. The six-state view consists of signed sums of 3 rows followed by 3 columns; evaluate against the supplied `-+- +-+` pattern. The 3-state view uses a binary indicator of positive occupancy on the center column, preserving `1 0 1` as expected pattern. The final one-state polarity is the sign of the 9-cell signed sum, with exact ties retained as unresolved 0 rather than silently assigning + or -. These are new decoding hypotheses; no historical key is implied.

Fixed v5 bond predicate: neighboring sites agree in >=6 of 8 core bits and >=6 of 8 shell bits. Then progressively require similarity >=6/9 in the nine, >=4/6 in six, >=2/3 in three, and same resolved polarity in one. Prescribed thresholds are model definitions, not evidence.
