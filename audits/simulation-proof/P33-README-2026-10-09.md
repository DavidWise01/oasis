## Conventional electromagnetic models

A: A constant, uniform scalar potential does not generate E or B. In vacuum, the photon has no frequency/wavelength shift from the *absolute potential*. The prior signed-charge phase is a gauge-dependent reference without a closed observable path.

B: A **potential difference** across electrodes and an electro-optic medium yields `E = V/gap`, `delta_n = -0.5 n^3 r overlap E`, `delta_phi = 2pi L delta_n/lambda_0`, at fixed vacuum laser wavelength. These material/geometry inputs are **not in the ROOT0 cipher** and constitute conventional phenomenological postulates, not newly discovered physical laws.

At the illustrative choice `V = -0.211 V, gap = 10 um, n = 2.2, effective r = 30 pm/V, L = 10 mm, overlap=1, input vacuum wavelength=600nm, visibility=0.8`:

- Electric field `-21,100 V/m`;
- Refractive index change `+3.370092e-6`;
- Material phase shift `+0.3529152089707245 rad`;
- Medium wavelength change `-0.4177793600 pm`;
- Vacuum wavelength change `0`, frequency change `0` (static medium);
- Illustrative biased interferometer intensity contrast `-0.1382539257` relative to the ideal null.

**Neither model follows uniquely from the ROOT0 208-symbol string.** A voltage with no gap or boundary-field specification cannot determine a wavelength. The Planck value is a display reference only.
