## Physical caution

The `n=0.5`, `κ=3.0` optical parameters, 30nm thickness, 10µm gap, 600nm probe, and surface `g` phase coefficient are **illustrative**, not measurements of this electrum. Ideal average centrosymmetric metal bulk has no conventional electric-dipole Pockels term; localized surface/structural responses may still occur. No direct wavelength conversion or Planck-scale descent from a −211mV static potential is inferred. This is a conditional mathematical simulator, not proof of simulation theory.
