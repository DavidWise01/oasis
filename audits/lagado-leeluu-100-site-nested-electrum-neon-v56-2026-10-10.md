## Explicit physical claims NOT made
- Neon is a noble gas. Twenty individual Ne atoms do not automatically create a continuous confining layer. A real condensed neon matrix generally requires cryogenic conditions and very many atoms; the literal 20-site shell is a model.
- Electrum is a variable-composition Au/Ag alloy, not a single periodic-table element or molecule. Forty alloy lattice sites could be modeled as Au_xAg_(40-x), with unspecified x. They cannot be assumed to form a real sealed atomic cage.
- Ten H and ten N represented as **individual atomic inventory slots** rather than stable isolated H and N shells, since free atomic H and N are reactive. O2 is an ordinary molecular representation, but the nested structure isn't demonstrated thermodynamically or chemically stable.
