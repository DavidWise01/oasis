## Mathematical boundary: spinor analogy, not spinor proof

For a **spin-1/2** spinor, the SU(2) rotation operator obeys `U(2π) = -I` and `U(4π) = I`. The 2π transformation changes the spinor sign, and two such rotations return it. An isolated overall minus phase is not itself a separately observable state; relative-phase interference can reveal the difference.

KELVIN uses **only the resulting two-step sign/return pattern** as its mnemonic indexing rule. It does not implement the full spinor representation, Pauli matrices, continuous rotations, amplitudes, measurement statistics or spacetime curvature. S/N are user-supplied markers, *not measured magnetic north/south poles*.

**Gravity waves** (buoyancy waves in fluids) differ from **gravitational waves** (propagating metric perturbations in general relativity); the supplied four-symbol alternation proves neither. There is no frequency, angular velocity, relativistic metric, signal carrier, gravitational coupling constant or elapsed-time law encoded in this model.
