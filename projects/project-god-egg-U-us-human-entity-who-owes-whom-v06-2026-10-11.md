The membership of U.us must be defined before an O.owed entry can have meaningful parties. Human = individuals/communities; Entity = institutions, organizations, public authorities, or systems, distinguished from their members. Humanity denotes the collective design scope, not an assertion of shared personal liability or equal capabilities.

## Core correction
**Who owes whom and why?** No blanket rule makes every human owe every other human. An alleged obligation needs:
- obligated actor or entity, with provenance,
- recipient or affected group,
- specific act, promise, law, benefit, adjudication, or other asserted basis,
- time period, causal link and evidence,
- legal/enforceable vs moral/political/proposed classification,
- amount and method if quantifiable, otherwise unknown,
- status (claimed, disputed, verified, discharged),
- allocation rule, consent/authority and avoidance of double counting.

Distinguish accountability of public/institutional entities from inherited personal guilt. Collective public programs can be funded by legitimate broadly applicable policies without implying every contributor individually committed the original harm. Historical inequality can justify deliberation on public remedy even where no presently adjudicated debtor exists.
