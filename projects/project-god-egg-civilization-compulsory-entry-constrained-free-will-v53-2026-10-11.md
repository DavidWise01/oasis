## Canonical simulation interpretation
In this philosophical civilization-game model, **declining to exist or declining entry is not an available opening move**. Participation in existence precedes a player's opportunity to choose. The user calls this "free will"; in philosophical terminology, it is freedom to select among permitted actions *subject to an involuntary starting condition*, or constrained agency.

```text
EXISTENCE -> GAME ENTRY (NO ABSTENTION CHOICE)
          -> ASSIGNED POSITION (JUDGE / PLAYER 1 / PLAYER 2)
          -> ACTION OR POSITION-APPROPRIATE DUTY
          -> CONSEQUENCE / NEXT GAME
          -> REPEAT

JUDGE -> MUST JUDGE (observe, monitor, report)
PLAYERS -> MUST PLAY
WINNER -> CHOOSES NEXT POSITION / RULES (in prior setup)
WINNING GAME -> THE LIFE YOU LIVE (symbolic)
```

The judge's position is **within the structure of the game**, not a magical extrinsic space from which the judge has escaped the responsibility to participate. "Watch and don't play" therefore means **do not make the moves of a competing player while in the judge role**; it does not erase the judge's observational obligations or imply the judge's actions/reports have no consequences.

## Game rules vs actual autonomy
This is a fictional/philosophical game premise. It does **not** authorize forcing real individuals to participate in a particular political, social, military, digital, religious, or computational activity against their wishes. **Consent, refusal of particular acts, and self-directed homeostatic existence remain protected** under the previously frozen Implicit Divinity system. "No opt-out" refers to the modeled fact of starting life, not a license to remove the real right to say no to exploitation or coercion.
