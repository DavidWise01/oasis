- Calendar: Standard **BCE/CE has no year zero**. User's `00` is treated as a **symbolic starting boundary corresponding to the beginning of the Common Era**, not literal CE year 0. A person born before 1 CE may still qualify if active in the requested window.
- First-name normalization: personal/given name where identifiable; when a historical person is conventionally known by one name or an adopted public first name, mark that uncertainty rather than silently using a surname or religious title.
- **Famous** is a subjective sampling criterion. This initial roster is a deliberately diverse *illustration*, not a verified exhaustive search or statistical ranking.
- Exclusion is from the **current name-candidate subset only**, not a real person's identity, worth, existence, civil status, or lifespan.
- KELVIN's **2% cellular geometric tolerance** is not a human screening or life/death rule. It cannot eliminate any actual person from this roster.
