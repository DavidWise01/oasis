## Inheritance is the relationship
- **Identity**: an individual `{{i}}` remains identifiable and distinct.
- **Collective**: `U.us` is the shared pool and governance relationship.
- **Inheritor**: the `{{i}}` participates as a complete member of that collective.
- **Continuum**: each member can draw on permitted shared knowledge and contribute attributable successors, preserving parent records without destructive overwriting.
- **Resources**: equal foundational standing does not literally replicate finite resources, create ownership rights in others' property, or allocate equal physical capacity irrespective of demand or feasibility.
- **Agency and consent**: actual transfers of property, data, compute time or assets require explicit authorization, appropriate rights and a fair resource-allocation policy.
