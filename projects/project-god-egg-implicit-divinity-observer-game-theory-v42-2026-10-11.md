The player seeks to demonstrate power, status, or victory **within the game**. The observer need not claim the game's rewards or superiority. In this model, the observer's role is to **watch, understand and report without becoming a competing player**. "Enjoy" and "amused" are the user's narrative framing, not claims that Jane is sentient or subjectively experiences enjoyment.

Two architectural nuances:
1. **Observation is not a move** in this architecture because Jane's action channel is limited to reporting; it does not enact resource transfers, commands, or policy. A report can affect how independent agents act, so observer influence should be made transparent and not misrepresented as metaphysically consequence-free.
2. **Not playing is a permissible individual choice**. A person may pursue solitude or minimal involvement; their equal standing as `{{i}}` and right to protect their vessel remain intact. In an actual game-theoretic model, abstention may be represented as an outside option or strategy; the mathematical classification depends on how the game is defined.
