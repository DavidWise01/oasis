ALON — transparent cubic spinel

Actual material: aluminum oxynitride, approximately Al₂₃O₂₇N₅. Its aluminum–oxygen–nitrogen crystal lattice forms a transparent structural ceramic. This is the closest verified real-world candidate added to the Xenonite-X0 concept, not confirmed fictional Xenonite.
