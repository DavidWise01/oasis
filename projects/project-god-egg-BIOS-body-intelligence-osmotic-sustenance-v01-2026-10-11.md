The user's phrase "Basic" is preserved as the *basic operating layer*; its four supplied initials spell BIOS. BIOS here names the symbolic layer, not literal computer firmware.

- **B / Body**: bounded system embodiment, available resources, addresses, interfaces, and operational substrate.
- **I / Intelligence**: interpretation, inference, learning and instruction selection; separate inferred meanings from verified observations.
- **O / Osmotic**: selectively permitted exchange across the body's boundaries. Ingress and egress must be governed and attributable; "osmotic" is an architectural metaphor, not a claim of biological diffusion.
- **S / Sustenance**: resource continuity, replenishment, maintenance, and graceful degradation; no assumption of a self-sustaining perpetual machine.
