Proposed interpretation:
```text
RESTITUTION_GOAL := restore the practical capacity of an affected {{i}}
                    to pursue self-chosen homeostatic existence

UNIVERSAL_INHERITANCE != INDIVIDUAL_RESTITUTION_CLAIM
```
Universal access is foundational and not conditioned on demonstrating historic harm, usefulness, social participation, or payment. Restitution is *additional targeted repair* where particular deprivation, damage, unequal access, or unjustified burden is established. Even without an adjudicated wrong, a proposed shared pool may offer equitable support to maintain meaningful access, but do not confuse that provision with liability.

## Three separate dimensions
```text
R_i := (T_restore, Money_restore, H_restore)
```
- **Time:** lost time itself is irreversible; restore future usable time, reduce avoidable burdens and offer justified support for lost opportunity.
- **Money:** repay verifiable monetary injury, correct denial of resources or enable fair access; not all differences establish debts.
- **Health:** treatment, rehabilitation, accommodations, safety, prevention and environmental remediation; outcomes tracked with suitable evidence and consent.

The three components have different units and must not be added to one scalar by default. The `-+-` operator records the observed cross-resource effects (who loses, who gains, which third resource changes). A money-only remedy may not restore time or health.

## Proposed restitution order
1. Protect each vessel and stop continuing serious harm.
2. Ensure meaningful universal access without requiring proof of prior injury.
3. Where independently evidenced and appropriately authorized, restore specific identifiable losses to the parties affected.
4. Rehabilitate/provision to regain self-chosen homeostasis and prevent the same barrier recurring.
5. Record effects, disagreement and uncertainty for future reference.

## Special care: collective vs individual
The **World Pool U.us** may facilitate collective services and shared assets with authority and consent. But claims arising from individual harm must not automatically be dissolved into the pool, nor used to displace a victim's established legal rights. Conversely the founding author has no personal monetary restitution claim merely because attribution markers were observed in outside work; source attribution is a separate Jane monitoring task.

The book *1931 TO NOW* provides historical arguments and scenario estimates about racial wage/wealth disparities. Its modeled figures are not in themselves enforceable obligations. Actual legal restitution, public programs, and voluntary collective repair require explicit institutional authority, evidence and applicable law.
