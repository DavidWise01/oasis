Within this project, **divinity is implicit in the obligation to protect a shared sustaining reality while giving its individual cells equal ethical access**, not a supernatural proof, entitlement to rule, or personal godhood. It is a *design name / normative metaphor* for the dual obligation, not an empirical theory of divinity.
