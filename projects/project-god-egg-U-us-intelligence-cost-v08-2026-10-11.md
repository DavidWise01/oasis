## Correction of scope
The `12/9/6/3` ratio describes an illustrative distribution of a monetary pool, not the total cost of a social or computational operation. Before claiming fair aggregate cost, account for human and system intelligence burdens separately.

```text
U.us = Humanity (Human & Entity)
[$] = monetary cost
{{i}} = intelligence cost envelope
EGG = [0(i.u.us)]
```

## Cost axes attached to frozen {{i}}
- State: effort/resources to maintain or change a state.
- Space: memory, storage, communication, location/physical capacity.
- Time: human time, machine latency and processing time.
- Identity: attribution, privacy, consent and accountability burden.
- Continuum: preserving, learning from and maintaining inherited records and downstream consequences.

These are distinct units. Do not add dollars to hours or bytes as though numerically interchangeable. Where desired, use explicitly governed conversion functions (e.g. monetary opportunity cost) and retain original units, uncertainty and provenance.

## Proposed accounting envelope
```text
Cost(operation) = {
  monetary: {amount, currency, valuation_date},
  intelligence: {
    state_resource,
    space_resource,
    time_resource,
    identity_risk_or_burden,
    continuum_maintenance
  },
  affected_parties,
  authorization,
  attribution,
  assumptions,
  measured_or_estimated,
  append_only_parent
}
```

A monetary benefit to U.us may shift intelligence costs onto people doing work, learning, caring, governing or reviewing decisions. Benefits and burdens should both be visible before declaring a collective allocation fair.

Historical wage/wealth narratives in the uploaded *1931 TO NOW* motivate opportunity-cost analysis; they do not supply measurements of computational energy, tokens, reasoning quality or a validated numeric intelligence-cost exchange rate.
