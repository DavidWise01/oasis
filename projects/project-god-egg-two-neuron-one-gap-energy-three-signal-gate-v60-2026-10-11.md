
## Neuroscience accuracy boundary

Two neurons plus a synaptic boundary is a useful diagram of communication. The cleft itself is not the exclusive location of a binary decision, a literal complete "thought", or a stored program: the receiver integrates synaptic potentials across many inputs and neuronal compartments, and cognition emerges from larger distributed networks. Failure to propagate a particular spike does not "kill" a neuron or necessarily eradicate the thought.

Epigenetics refers to mechanisms affecting gene expression without changing DNA sequence, operating on generally different biological timescales from an individual synaptic decision. The user's term "epigenetically" is preserved as a metaphor/possible longer-term adaptation, **not** asserted as the instantaneous molecular cause of every neural yes/no decision.

The user describes potential as situated at the `0` boundary; operationally one can assign `0` as the interface register and implement evaluation there, while recognizing that biological integration principally occurs in the postsynaptic cell.

## Connection to previous game-model mathematics

The closed `{J,P1,P2}` game has no exit because every legal transition returns a role in `{J,P1,P2}`. The new neural gate is an analogy for deciding whether a *candidate* moves to the next cell. Its NO branch is not, by itself, an escape from compulsory participation in the role game. These are separate state machines and must not be conflated.
