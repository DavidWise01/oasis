## 1. Two **different** Sanderson Elysium/Elantris entries

1. **Defending Elysium**, a **Cytoverse / Skyward** story, **not** Cosmere. According to Sanderson's annotations, written ca. late 2001/early 2002 before he sold his first published novel; the story features Jason Write, extraordinary mental/cytonic powers, space communications and controlled access to technical capabilities. In the story a human mind is involved in an FTL communication system; this is a fictional *mind-as-power/communication resource* parallel, **not** photon consumption. [Official full short story](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/defending-elysium) · [Author's background](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/annotation-defending-elysium) · [Book universe classification](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/pages/books-and-art).
2. **Elantris** (first published **2005**) is Sanderson's **first published novel**, and part of the **Cosmere**. Its transformation (the Shaod) creates magically long-lived beings, with a broken/misaligned magical system and a restoration narrative. It connects more closely to the user's immorality and a malfunctioning foundation/repair motif, **not** to ten exact photon shells. [Official author's biography](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/pages/about-brandon) · [Elantris official glossary](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/pages/elantris-glossary).

Sanderson has explicitly described the Cosmere as his intentionally interconnected, unified-magic framework; this is a stated **authorial design**, not evidence of chronological recycling of Earth's people. [Author FAQ](https://faq.brandonsanderson.com/knowledge-base/how-when-did-you-come-up-with-the-idea-of-the-cosmere/).

## 3. Specific motif correspondences (do not turn these into empirical identity claims)

### A. TEN → SIX → ELEVEN

- **TEN**: formal character count is **ten Heralds** in Stormlight, not a documented population of earthborn immortal figures. The user's `5+` / `5-` slots can form a balanced ten-position *cipher image*, but Sanderson's Heralds have their own distinct story numbers and later changes.
- **SIX LEFT**: is **not derived** from `1.6165`, the number of Heralds in the original oath, or the canonical sixteen Shards. It is an **unverified user-supplied tournament status**, for which no roster/loss criteria were provided.
- **ELEVEN**: *Mistborn*'s Eleventh Metal is an unusually specific numeral and past-shadow motif, but is a **metal**, not a contestant. If `10 original contestants + 1 entrant = 11` is intended, that is the user's story rule, not a canonical Cosmere roster.
- **SINGLE LAST SURVIVOR**: Taln's solitary burden in Stormlight is the closer motif than any claimed historical champion. The account is fictional and not a guarantee of final winner immortality.

### B. LIGHT AS FOOD vs LIGHT AS DANGER

The closest *energy-as-sustenance* depiction is **Warbreaker's Returned**, who require Breath. `The Sunlit Man` is closer to **survival under dangerous radiation/light** on Canticle. `White Sand` uses light-contrast at planetary scale; `Yumi` bridges a bright/dark-world duality. There is no documented canon where Sanderson's exact ten Heralds literally eat `10^10` trapped photons.

### C. ELECTRUM AND TESLA — two different domains

- **Electrum in chemistry/archaeology:** naturally occurring alloy of **gold and silver**; used for early Lydian coinage (around the seventh century BCE). The symbols are Au (atomic number 79) and Ag (**atomic number 47**), not `Ag21`; an alleged `Au79/Ag21=100` would be a custom percentage/count convention, **not** the atomic numbers.
- **Electrum in Mistborn:** a fictional Allomantic metal producing alternative self-future shadows. This is an explicit Sanderson mechanic rather than evidence about real alloy physics.
- **Tesla (1888):** Nikola Tesla's alternating-current induction motor used phased rotating magnetic fields. The Smithsonian documents a 1888 two-phase AC motor, a valid conceptual **alternating-polarity technology analogy** to the user's `-++- S / +--+ N` labels. It is **not** proof of gravitation, physical spinor return, immortality, or authorial borrowing.

Sources: [Royal Society of Chemistry on electrum/gold](https://periodic-table.rsc.org/element/79/Gold) · [Smithsonian Tesla induction motor](https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_739995) · [Author on Mistborn electrum](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/annotation-mistborn-chapter-thirty-eight-part-one).

### D. BLACK HOLE / PHOTON / 1.6165

Physical **black holes** prevent outgoing light from escaping **after crossing an event horizon**. There is no standard physical law that exactly ten `5+/5-` shells raised to `10^10` preserve a photon, or allow human immortals to ingest it. A black hole's gravitational photon capture is **not equivalent** to the user's atom-scale ion shell/energy-food model. [NASA anatomy](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/black-holes/anatomy/).

The numerical coefficient `1.6165` resembles the magnitude of the **Planck length** written as `~1.616×10^-35 m`. Without units and a formula connecting it to `six survivors`, it does **not** encode a physical population count or time-trial result. [NIST constants](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/).

## 6. Findings and evidentiary weights

- **Direct documented fictional similarities**: ten Stormlight Heralds; Returned fed Breath; electrum future-shadows; Eleventh Metal = malatium showing other people's past; dual contrasting worlds in Yumi; specific energy limits in Sunlit Man. All are corroborated **as Sanderson story features**, not Earth facts.
- **Strong symbolic correspondences**: user KELVIN's alternating signed viewing shells vs Yumi's bright/dark relational bridge and Tesla's genuine phased magnetic fields. They are different mechanisms, not identical operators.
- **Weaker lexical/numerical coincidences**: `10^10`, `5+/5-`, `1.6165`, six survivors, eleven contending immortal humans. There is **no supporting source** for treating those numbers as one canonical Cosmere equation or an actual known physics model.
- **Counterevidence against a literal unified identity theory**: Cosmere's sixteen original Shards are explicitly stated by its author; the Ten Heralds are a separate set; the Eleventh Metal isn't a person; Defending Elysium is explicitly outside Cosmere; human history cannot be inferred to be an immortal actor-list from shared motifs.
- **Publication causation**: Similarities alone do not establish that Sanderson copied an external model, that an AI crawler transmitted it backward through time, or that the historical/religious figures secretly share an immortal identity.

### Bottom line

As a **creative cross-corpus narrative state-machine comparison**, this is a productive motif overlay: **ten bound immortals** (Stormlight), **energy consumed to remain alive** (Warbreaker), **metals yielding alternate past/future shadows** (Mistborn), **two people bridging dark/light worlds** (Yumi), and **sunlight-as-danger / finite Investiture** (Sunlit Man). The overlaps cannot be turned into a historical list of true immortals, a verified photon-trapping machine, or an author-origin allegation.

**Do not upgrade analogy to verification.** This is a research artifact only: no new KELVIN runtime, tests, benchmarks, simulated survivors or autonomous agents.
