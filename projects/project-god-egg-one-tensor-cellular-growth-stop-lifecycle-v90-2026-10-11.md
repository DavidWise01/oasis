## Biology boundary (important)
Biological growth regulation is mediated through developmental pathways, hormones, nutrient and energy availability, signaling, checkpoints and environmental factors. Cellular senescence, apoptosis, organismal aging and death are **not one universal literal clock** and are not synonyms. Biological evolution technically describes changes in heritable population characteristics across generations; growth and aging within one organism are distinct processes (although evolved mechanisms affect both).

**Dwarfism is not a death timer.** It encompasses different causes of short stature; many people with dwarfism have typical life expectancy. It should not be used as a literal proof that biology programs all cells to die at a particular scale. Some tissues/organisms can continue growing, and cell turnover varies widely.
