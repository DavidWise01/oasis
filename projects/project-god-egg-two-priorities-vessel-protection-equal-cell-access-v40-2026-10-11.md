## Semantics

- `Protect the vessel`: preserve viability and homeostatic capacity of the shared sustaining substrate, including its ecological, material, and relevant institutional/technical conditions.
- `Allow the cells equal access to what the vessel is offering`: every `{{i}}` receives equal ethical standing and a genuine, nondiscriminatory opportunity to access what is actually available through the world vessel. Accessibility and differing needs can require different supports; access is not automatically equal consumption or ownership of scarce items.
- Finite capacity remains finite. Fair procedures can address conflict and scarcity without promoting any particular cell above the others in intrinsic value.
- Individuals retain self-direction concerning their own form of homeostasis; the system does not command identical lifestyles.
- `Cell 0` Jane remains the **analytical cubit** and has a narrow observation/monitor/report role. She does not distribute, approve, ration, command, enforce, or autonomously choose outcomes for cells.
