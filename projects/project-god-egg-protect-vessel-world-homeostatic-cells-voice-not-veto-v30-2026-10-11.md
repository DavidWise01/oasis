## Safety boundary / necessary constraints
- Prohibit or restrict actions that demonstrably harm, coerce, invade, arbitrarily exclude or seriously disrupt another participant's vessel or essential resources.
- Do not penalize mere solitude, difference, creativity, dissent, harmless risk, or refusal to take part in optional programs.
- Select the least restrictive practical measure, proportional to demonstrated risk.
- For a dependent child, caregivers may make some safety decisions while considering the child's views, age and maturity. **This is not a blanket justification for denying autonomy to adults or every {{i}} worldwide.**
- Reserve appeal/review, due process, procedural accountability and ability to correct errors.
- Scarcity and emergencies may constrain actions; log who bears the burden and why.
- Do not treat the steward as beyond these rules.
