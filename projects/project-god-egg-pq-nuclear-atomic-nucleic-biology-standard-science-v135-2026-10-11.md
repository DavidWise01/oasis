## 3. Two distinct N labels are essential

- `N1 / NUCLEAR`: atomic nucleus, protons/neutrons, interaction lengths of order femtometers (`10^-15 m`). The residual strong force stabilizes many nuclei. This is a physics problem **smaller than atoms**.
- `N2 / NUCLEIC`: DNA and RNA, large molecular polymers with genetic and regulatory roles, **larger than atoms** and assembled from nucleotides. The B-DNA double helix diameter is **about 2 nm = 2 × 10^-9 m**; 0.34 nm axial rise per base pair. These are molecular-biological mechanisms and do not claim nuclear-force confinement of DNA.

**Nuclear ≠ Nucleic ≠ cell nucleus.** A cellular nucleus is a membrane-enclosed organelle in eukaryotes, yet another sense of the term and a much larger structure.
