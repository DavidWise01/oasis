## 1. Working interpretation — specify what has and hasn't been assigned

**Domain in this table means letter domain L/M/N/O/P** based on the prior cohort request. This is a provisional interpretation, **NOT** the same as the separate **eight environmental domains** in the user's game architecture. No mapping of five letter labels onto eight domain ports has been provided.

The four **user-provided** roles are `-m`, `+m`, `-f`, `+f` **per letter**, yielding 20 deceased predecessors and 20 matched younger biographical examples, for **40 distinct named people total**. As a practical convention compatible with the previous screens, `-` means a **first-name initial**, `+` means a **last-name initial**, and `m/f` is a **public male/female presentation role** for selecting examples, not a genetic finding or metaphysical property. The sides do **not** mean good or bad.

**Eligibility:** predecessor born in inclusive **1900–1980** and documented deceased by 2026-10-11; comparison candidate born in inclusive **1966–1976**, matching the same letter and role and, where practical, similar public professional work. The user has **not** required the younger person's birth to occur *after* the older person's death, and many pairs overlap in lifespan. This is therefore a *symbolic address match*, **not** a proposed literal transmigration chain.


Only **two** pairings have the predecessor deceased before the counterpart was born: **Louis Armstrong (1971) → Liam Gallagher (1972)** and **Marilyn Monroe (1962) → Minnie Driver (1970)**. The **other 18 pairs have overlapping lifetimes** (the younger person was already born before the older person died). **This itself rules out a strict “older person dies, then same unique biological life is reborn” succession for most rows.** Even for the other two, chronological succession is not evidence of reincarnation.

All matches were chosen **after seeing names, dates and letter roles**. As such, obtaining 20 out of 20 matches in this deliberately populated table is **not** a statistical validation of a hidden real-world mechanism. To test an empirical hypothesis, the underlying population, mapping and selection rule would need to be fixed *before* examining outcomes.
