## What a commodity means in this symbolic engine
`[$C$]` marks a value-bearing offering with a defined **transfer/exchange interface**; it could represent a good or eligible tradable service within the project's broad architectural use of "commodity." In narrower economic terminology, not every priced service or distinct asset is a standardized commodity; classification remains explicit. A tradable claim requires an authorized holder, exchange terms, and a counterpart.

```text
Commodity {
  commodity_id,
  origin_value_record: vector_0000_reference,
  offering: good | service | licensed_right | other_lawful_tradable_claim,
  description_and_unit,
  provider_or_seller,
  authorized_owner_or_rights_holder,
  buyer_or_recipient,
  price: {currency, amount, price_date} | not_set,
  exchange_terms,
  evidence_of_delivery_or_transfer,
  time_cost,
  money_cost,
  health_impact: observed | estimated | unknown,
  i_address: {State,Space,Time,Identity,Continuum},
  lineage_parent
}
```

**Value is not the same as price.** A participant may value something that is not transferable, and declaring value does not itself create ownership, scarcity, a lawful right to sell it, or a willing buyer. The engine should distinguish:
- value identified;
- exchange rights established;
- offer/pricing specified;
- voluntary, lawful agreement reached;
- transfer/delivery recorded.

No price, value, or monetary return is invented. The marketplace remains a design surface, not a functioning marketplace.

## Inviolable boundary
People, their dignity, personhood, consent, and identity are **not made saleable commodities** by this symbolic transformation. A human may voluntarily sell legitimate products or authorized labor/services under applicable rights, but `{{i}}` itself remains an attributable identity rather than market property. Health effects stay independently recorded and must not be silently priced away.
