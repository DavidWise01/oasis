### Distinguish three things
- **Existence**: An agent's starting condition was not selected before birth. This is the user's earlier "you have to play" paradox.
- **Role**: Once assigned a player or judge position under the simulation, the agent may have obligations in that particular round.
- **Participation**: The newly clarified long-run *win condition* is the ability to decide whether to join further games or not, without being trapped in a continuing compulsory position.

This corrects the v5.3 implication that *compulsory participation is permanently the essence of free will*. Instead, the user distinguishes involuntary initial entry from the subsequent attainment of freedom to participate or decline.
