- **Observer position = 3D** for this observation. Do not move the observer to 2D or 4D by implication.
- Previously defined **`−` / `+`** are two complementary orientations/views, **not** GOOD/BAD.
- Preserve **G20 / I3**, **1971–1976**, **two roots**, **`13 × 1 × 13 = 169` address positions**, **A–Z = 26 mnemonic identity/name labels**.
- Names are **memory indexes / carriers**, not automatically the personally known historical individuals illustrated in v11.9; no person is demonstrated to be immortal, divine or a demigod.
- The prior **two gods support ten split-power roles** is a *different register* from 26 mnemonic carriers and 169 addresses. The carrier count does **not** overwrite or expand the ten-role power capacity.
