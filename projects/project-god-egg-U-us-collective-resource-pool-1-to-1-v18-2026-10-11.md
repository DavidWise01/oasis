- Each human has the **same fundamental claim to participation and fair access**: `person_i : person_j = 1 : 1`.
- This is an equality-of-standing rule, **not** arithmetic multiplication of finite goods and not an assertion that each participant already owns all privately held physical assets.
- Voluntary consent, scarcity, accessibility accommodations, differing needs, physical feasibility, and lawful custodianship govern how actual resources can be provisioned. When a resource is rivalrous, define transparent allocation and capacity, without reducing anyone's foundational standing.
- Human and Entity have distinct operational roles; entities can build, maintain, steward, contribute, and administer resources subject to governance and accountability. An entity's access permissions are not automatically the same as a human's dignity/rights.
