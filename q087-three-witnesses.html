Frozen invariants

  • No quorum: neither 2-of-3 nor 3-of-3.
  • No voting: observations cannot elect a conclusion.
  • No consensus gate: contradictory reports remain valid records.
  • No report suppression: uncertain, silent and conflicting observations are preserved.
  • One interpreting cortex: {{i}} alone evaluates consequences and selects an action.
  • Unbounded logical memory: {{m-.00 |> INF <| +.00}}; finite physical instantiation.
