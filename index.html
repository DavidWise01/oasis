Feng Shui is represented here as a historical and cultural spatial-design tradition. Practical ideas such as circulation, orientation, daylight, visual protection, clutter reduction, balance, accessibility, and occupant preference can be evaluated directly. Qi or auspicious mappings are not presented as experimentally established physical quantities.
