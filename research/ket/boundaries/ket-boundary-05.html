Boundary distinctions

A hash chain can be rebuilt by anyone who controls its storage. A signature adds evidence only when the public key is authenticated via a separately held pin. This demonstration does not prove the truth of the recorded governance evidence, identify a legal signer, or deliver a packet to an external service.

Checks: source schema and canonical token hash, all receipt hashes and graph routes, unique packet IDs, ECDSA P-256 signature, trusted-key pin, checkpoint prefix and truncation. Extra journal entries after the checkpoint are permissible but remain unanchored unless a later signed checkpoint is supplied.
