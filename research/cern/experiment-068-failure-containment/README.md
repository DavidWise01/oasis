# PRIM 068 — Failure containment

Sp0 begins failed with 0.050 J. A fraction t is retained in `||[;]||`; remainder is divided equally between Sp1 and Sp2. Failure threshold is 0.020 J per sector inclusive. Exact rational checks pass for t=0,0.20,0.50,0.60,0.80,1.00. At 0% and 20% both neighbors fail; from 50% onwards neither fails. Exact threshold: t>20% avoids **immediate** secondary failure. Failed-neighbor energy is credited to dissipation as a modeling convention; no downstream cascade, physical absorption, or actual quantum shell is proven. No leader/quorum.
