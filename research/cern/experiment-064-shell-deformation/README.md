# PRIM 064 — Elastic shell under outward pulse

Canonical compression boundary `||\/|| → ||[;]||` with a classical quasistatic spring surrogate. 3 stiffnesses × 3 deformation limits × 5 pulse energies × 3 areas = 135 cases and **675/675** invariant checks. 57 cases reach/exceed capacity. In exact rational arithmetic, `E_stored=min(E_pulse,k*xmax²/2)` and `E_reflected=E_pulse-E_stored`, so energy closes. Verify squared deflection remains within bound and pressure scale `k*xmax/A` maps back to restoring force.

Assumptions: no dynamic kinetic energy, absorption or dissipation, no transmission, no microscopic field interactions, no actual Planck-length shell physics. Excess energy reflection is assigned by the model. The extended downloadable test and its JSON dataset are in `prim_exp064.zip`. No quorum or leader.
