    Rules on this board
    • Six boxes begin gh / ab / mn / ab / mn / ab. Their frames alternate eo / oe.
    • Letters have numbers a=1 … z=26. This version reads +_1 as a player-selected +1 or −1.
    • One X per four slots: boxes 1+2, 3+4, 5+6. Scan from the upper left down, then the right column.
    • Equal numbers on the top, bottom, left or right edge ablate together. Diagonal matches do not.
    • A vertical ablation removes one above and one below. Press Compress _ to close column gaps upward while preserving order; scanning restarts at the top.
    • Horizontal ablations leave holes. Read surviving letters down the left column, then down the right.
    • The goal is one compound, gi. Two live numbers must actually be 7,9. No automatic renaming.
    • The register records moves and replays them on reload. New round adds a restart entry. Export keeps a portable copy.
    A route from this starting stack

    Keep the first g; move its h up once to i. In each ab box, move a up to b, then ablate that horizontal pair. In each mn box, move n down to m, then ablate. The remaining letters read gi.

    Try vertical matches too: they trigger the compression rule.
