Interpretation and public record

Inner-Galaxy mismatch: The model overpredicts 5.27–7.22 kpc rotation speeds. The flagged count is a transparent heuristic, not a statistical confidence test: bin errors and systematic uncertainties are correlated, the source provides two separate uncertainty columns, and original studies adopt slightly different Galactic-center distance conventions.

The original 729 stations stop at the Sun (8.277 kpc); the 31 external-bin predictions beyond that boundary are clearly identified extrapolations of the same analytic potential. Observations at radii smaller than 5 kpc are deliberately not claimed because Eilers excludes the Galactic bar region from its axisymmetric analysis.
