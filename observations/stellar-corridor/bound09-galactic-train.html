All station positions share the same common translation. The intermediate stations are reference samples of the center-to-Sun ray, not dynamically integrated stars. Their 729 frozen midplane field values are unchanged. The Sun's circular track is an approximation, and Earth's separate 1 AU orbit is negligible at kiloparsec resolution. The slider's 320 km/s reference speed is hypothetical; it is not claimed as a measured intrinsic Sgr A* or Milky Way bulk velocity.
