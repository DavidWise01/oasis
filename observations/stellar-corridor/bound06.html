Do not treat radius-only transformations as recalculated astrometry. A proper change of the Sun–Galactic-Center distance also changes transformed stellar coordinates, velocities and adopted solar motion. The H I curves assume R₀ = 8.122 kpc and Θ₀ = 233.34 km/s. No H I uncertainty covariance was available in these tabulations, so no gas-tracer significance test is claimed.

Three independent observations · no quorum

A — red giants: Jeans-inferred circular velocities from APOGEE/Gaia-era Eilers 2019. B — neutral hydrogen: terminal-velocity derived quadrant curves with noncircular streaming and differing assumptions. C — model: unchanged axisymmetric gravitational potential, 5/60/35 bulge/disk/halo normalized at Sun. The witnesses need not agree.
