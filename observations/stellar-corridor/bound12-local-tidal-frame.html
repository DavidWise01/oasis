The analytic Jacobi factors solve the linearized geodesic-deviation equations for initial unit separations with zero initial relative velocity. Their accuracy fails for a finite cloud when the displacement is no longer small compared with local geometric scales. The 1% gate is explicitly user-selectable model hygiene (held fixed in this version), **not** a measured tearing threshold. A free-falling ideal reference observer has zero proper acceleration; only nearby freely falling points accelerate relative to one another.
