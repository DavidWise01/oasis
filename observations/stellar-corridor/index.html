Data coverage and limitations

Not a completed 27,000-light-year census. These real stars are selected from the 9,096-star bright-star table derived from the Yale Bright Star Catalogue and Hipparcos. This sample is strongly biased toward the solar neighborhood. All 729 chosen stars lie within approximately 741 light-years in projected distance from the Sun along the target line, leaving a large unmapped interval. The selection radius is data-derived (~34.8 pc); angular positions alone cannot fill that gap. These records do not contain validated masses, so they cannot establish gravitational contributions.

Method: for each star with estimated heliocentric distance d and sky unit vector s, project on the Sgr A* direction u: along = d (s · u); offset = d √(1 − (s · u)²). Retain stars with 0 < along < 8277 pc, select 729 with smallest perpendicular offset, order from Sgr A* toward Sun. The physical sky direction is RA 266.41683°, Dec −29.00781°. Uniform stations are generated independently every ~37.1 ly, with no invented star identity.
