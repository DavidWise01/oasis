This coupled solver is physically constrained only within a deliberately small interior patch (r near 1.2, Q=0.8, M₀=1 in geometric units). It does not approach the inner Cauchy horizon, propagate Price tails to arbitrarily late advanced time, or model a rotating black hole. Convergence alone does not establish that such additional regimes have been solved.
