Physical containment

Observation layer: Q1 and Q4 velocities derive from neutral-hydrogen terminal-velocity analysis with specified solar reference assumptions. Only five deliberately selected source pairs are compared; published gas tables lack usable uncertainty covariance in this extract.

Hypothetical layer: δΦ = εv₀² f(R) cos[2(φ−φbar)], where f(R)=(R/4.5kpc)² exp[1−(R/4.5kpc)²]. This gives radial acceleration −∂δΦ/∂R and azimuthal acceleration −(1/R)∂δΦ/∂φ. The bar amplitude is not fitted to the five gas points and the perturbation does not preserve the earlier solar calibration when ε is nonzero.

Boundary: a difference in inferred tangential velocity between two gas quadrants cannot uniquely determine an additional gravitational force. Noncircular streaming, bar/spiral dynamics, distinct sightlines and correlated measurement uncertainty remain unresolved. The point-mass calculation is undefined at the black hole center.
