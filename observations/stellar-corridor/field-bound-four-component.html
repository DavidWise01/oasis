Boundary rules

Model components: bulge (cutoff power law, exponent 1.8, cutoff radius 1.9 kpc); disk (Miyamoto–Nagai, a = 3.0 kpc, b = 0.28 kpc); dark halo (NFW, scale radius 16 kpc); and Sgr A* (a separate 4.297-million-solar-mass Newtonian point source).

At the Sun, the *non-black-hole remainder* is partitioned into 5% bulge, 60% disk, and 35% halo radial contributions. This matches the original model's local weights, but is not a direct measurement of these separate mass fractions.

The inner-bulge profile, dark halo, and all 729 radial values are extrapolations. Station 1 at Sgr A*'s mathematical center is undefined. No measured stellar masses, gas clouds, Galactic bar, spiral arms, actual gravity vectors off the plane, or inferred stellar-mass errors are included. No mass-gap or Yang–Mills result is inferred.
