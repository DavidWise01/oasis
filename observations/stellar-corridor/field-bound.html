Quarter rule · distance, not body size

For the same mass, double the distance from the black hole and the acceleration drops to one quarter. Likewise, a different point mass with ¼ the mass at ½ the distance produces the same magnitude, though vector direction still matters.

No physical mass, velocity, or gravitational contribution is inferred for any of the 729 original catalog stars. Sgr A* contributes approximately 0.0041% of the inward acceleration indicated by the Sun's ~233 km/s galactic circular speed. Most comes from extended galactic mass, including gas, stars and dark matter.
